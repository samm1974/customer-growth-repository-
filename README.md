## Customer Retention Case Study

*This business and its data are fictional.*

### Business Overview

Everyday Fitness Video offers workout videos through a monthly subscription. Its customers are adults looking for convenient workouts they can do at home.

### Monthly Business Information

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Monthly subscription price | $30 |
| Subscribers at the beginning of the month | 200 |
| Subscribers who canceled | 15 |
| New subscribers | 10 |
| Subscribers at the end of the month | 195 |

### Revenue and Retention Analysis

- Starting monthly recurring revenue: **$6,000**
- Monthly recurring revenue lost to cancellations: **$450**
- Monthly recurring revenue from new subscribers: **$300**
- Ending monthly recurring revenue: **$5,850**
- Net monthly recurring revenue decline: **$150**
- Subscriber churn rate: **7.5%**
- Gross revenue retention: **92.5%**

These calculations assume everyone pays $30, with no discounts, upgrades or downgrades. Gross revenue retention excludes new subscribers.

### My Assessment

The business lost more subscribers than it gained. New sales helped offset cancellations, but monthly recurring revenue still declined.

The numbers show what happened. They do not yet explain why customers canceled.

### What I Would Investigate

- Cancellation reasons and customer feedback
- Viewing activity before cancellation
- Whether cancellations
